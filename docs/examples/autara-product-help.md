# Autara product help (demo document)

> This is a **product help sheet about how the Autara app works**. It is not clinical guidance, contains no medical facts, and is only meant for testing the reference-document pipeline. Real clinical sources must be approved by your team.

## Screening in Autara
Autara offers the M-CHAT-R questionnaire for children aged 16 to 30 months. A caregiver answers 20 yes or no questions, one per screen, and can go back to change an answer. At the end Autara shows a risk tier (lower, medium or higher) and lists which answers counted toward it. The result is a screening aid and is not a diagnosis. Autara always asks the caregiver to discuss results with a qualified clinician.

## Who sees what
Caregivers see their own child's screening result straight away. A clinician on the child's care team can review the screening, add notes, change the risk tier with a written reason, and mark it as reviewed. After review, the caregiver sees the final tier and the clinician's reason. Notes written by a clinician stay inside the care team.

## Behaviour log
The behaviour log records three parts of an event: what happened just before, what the child did, and what happened right after. A caregiver or therapist also gives an intensity from 1 to 5 and can add the time, how long it lasted, and where it happened. Autara then shows charts such as entries per week, behaviours by type, time of day, and which events tend to come before which behaviours. Please avoid typing names into the notes.

## Care team
A caregiver adds a therapist or clinician to the care team by typing their email address. The person must already have an Autara account that an administrator has verified. Everyone on the care team can see that child's screenings and behaviour logs. The caregiver can remove someone from the care team at any time.

## Reports
Once a screening has been reviewed by a clinician, the caregiver can download a report as a PDF or a CSV file from the Reports page. The report shows the child's initials and age in months instead of the full name, and every page carries the reminder that it is a screening aid and not a diagnosis.

## AI summaries
Clinicians can ask Autara to draft an explanation of a screening. The draft points to the answers and behaviour log entries it is based on and states what it is unsure about. Caregivers only see an AI-drafted summary after a clinician has approved it.

## Ask Autara
Ask Autara answers general questions using only the reference documents that your team has loaded. It shows the sources it used. If the documents do not contain an answer it says it does not know. It does not give medical advice, medication advice or a diagnosis. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.
